The Golden Triangle

Three countries, two rivers, one long golden afternoon.

น้ำรวกบรรจบน้ำโขง ไทย ลาว เมียนมา มาพบกันตรงนี้เจ้า

Sop Ruak

Where the Ruak meets the Mekong

ยามแลงแดดสีทองส่องน้ำโขง งามขนาดเน้อ

Douglas Perkins · Commons · CC BY 4.0

“One of the most beautiful places in Thailand. It was the highlight of our visit, and we went back several times.”

An hour and a little north of Chiang Rai, past Mae Chan and the turn for Chiang Saen, the road comes down to the water and stops. Across the Mekong is Laos. Up the smaller river, the Ruak, is Myanmar. The bend between them is the Golden Triangle, and in the late afternoon the name explains itself: the light goes gold, the river goes gold, and so does the great seated Buddha on the bank.

Plan on the whole afternoon. Start at the Hall of Opium, walk the river road to the viewpoint, take a longtail out onto the Mekong, and finish on the glass walkway south of Chiang Saen with the sun going down behind the hills.

Sop Ruakสบรวก

The village at the confluence. Stand on the viewing terrace and the three countries sit in front of you across the water: Myanmar up the Ruak, Laos across the Mekong.

The Golden Buddhaพระพุทธนวล้านตื้อ

A seated golden Buddha on a ship-shaped platform at the water's edge, the landmark of the bend. Go late in the afternoon, when the gold and the river match.

The Hall of Opiumหอฝิ่น

The great museum of the valley's history, run by the Mae Fah Luang Foundation, in the park above Sop Ruak. The painted panels of the farming year are worth the visit on their own. Leave time for the gift shop.

Map

The House of Opiumบ้านฝิ่น

The small one, in Sop Ruak village: a private museum opened in 1989 by Patcharee Srimattayakul, born in Chiang Saen. Scales, weights, pipes and the stories behind them, and a gift shop of its own. Open early to late.

Coffee and crafts, from the makersกาแฟดอย · งานฝีมือชาวเขา

Along the river road and up at the viewpoints, hill-village families sell their own coffee and their own weaving and embroidery, with the confluence below you. Buy from the person who made it.

The glass walkwayสกายวอล์ค

South of Chiang Saen a glass floor reaches out over the treetops toward the Mekong, with blossom trees and an arch of flowers at the end. Bring a hat; the late sun is gold.

Chiang Saenเชียงแสน

The old Lanna river city, with its brick walls and moat still standing among the trees. Wat Pa Sak, from 1295, sits in a teak grove on the western edge.

Map

A longtail on the Mekongล่องเรือแม่น้ำโขง

Boats leave from the landings at Sop Ruak for a loop of the three banks. Twenty minutes on the water is the best view of the triangle there is.
